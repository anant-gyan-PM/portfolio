## Anant's Perspective on AI and Fintech Infrastructure
Anant is a Product Operations Manager with five years of experience building fintech infrastructure the conventional way, across crypto exchanges and fintech platforms — the infrastructure layer most PMs never touch: KYC/AML, custody, reconciliation, and compliance workflows. That work produced results including 70,000+ users across the US, Asia, and Europe, $3M+ in secure asset custody, a 0.2% fee leakage caught through real-time reconciliation, and a CSAT lift to 4.85 after redesigning KYC onboarding end-to-end. Now that AI is part of the toolkit, he sees that earlier infrastructure work differently — as a landscape full of untapped potential for AI to optimize, replace, or entirely rebuild.

## Why Financial Literacy Is Where Anant Sees the Fintech Gap
Anant's core belief, shaped by years inside fintech infrastructure: financial products fail at the logic layer, not the surface. That led him to a conclusion — the people who most need trustworthy financial infrastructure are exactly the ones fintech has historically never built for. Financial literacy is where that gap lives. He brought that thesis to Asset Builders of America, where he now builds a financial literacy platform for rural communities, tribal nations, and underserved school districts across Wisconsin, with AI at the center of how the work gets done — 70+ teacher interviews across 50+ school districts synthesized into product decisions in real time, prototypes and user flows generated and tested the same week research happened, $250K in funding secured, and the Wisconsin Governor's Award for Financial Literacy.

## Where Anant Sees AI Belonging in Fintech Infrastructure
Anant's current thinking centers on where AI genuinely belongs inside the infrastructure fintech companies are building: compliance systems, KYC/AML flows, and reconciliation logic. He thinks in concrete terms — what AI can optimize, what it can replace entirely, and what it would rebuild from the ground up if given the chance — and believes most of that potential in fintech is still untouched. During his MBA, working on an AI-powered SQL optimization engine with Google mentors reinforced this lens: the team didn't just speed up an existing process, they rebuilt what it was actually doing, achieving dramatically faster query execution. That's the distinction Anant draws between optimizing what exists and restructuring what's possible — and it's the gap between today's fintech infrastructure and what it could be with AI genuinely embedded in the logic layer that keeps him engaged, grounded in having lived inside that infrastructure himself.
