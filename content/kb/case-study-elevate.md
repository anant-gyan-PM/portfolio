## ELEVATE Wisconsin — The Challenge
Members of tribal nations receive substantial distributions ($100,000+) upon turning 18. Without proper financial education, many waste these resources on impulsive purchases or fall victim to predatory lending schemes. Rural and tribal communities lack accessible financial literacy programs integrated into school curricula, creating a critical gap in financial preparedness for young adults.

## ELEVATE Wisconsin — The Solution
ELEVATE Wisconsin provides comprehensive financial education content and structured teacher training programs to integrate financial literacy into K-12 curricula. Rather than building a direct-to-student platform, the team recognized that teachers are the true gatekeepers of curriculum adoption — the platform trains teachers to become financial education advocates, creating a multiplier effect across school districts. This ensures sustainability and cultural relevance in rural and tribal communities.

## ELEVATE Wisconsin — Go-to-Market Strategy
The GTM strategy centered on three pillars: (1) Advisory Board Validation — spent 6 months building relationships with educators, tribal leaders, financial experts, and state government officials, co-creating the program to address real needs. (2) Structured Pilots — ran 3-month pilots with 5 school districts, measuring teacher engagement, student outcomes, and operational feasibility as proof points for scaling. (3) Multiplier Effect — trained teachers became advocates who trained other staff, creating organic growth within districts without additional marketing spend.

## ELEVATE Wisconsin — Key Achievements
$250K in funding secured. Projected reach of 8,000+ individuals. 20+ school districts engaged. Rollout scoped across 1,500+ departments in Wisconsin. Built AI-powered workflows that cut operational overhead 60% by automating grant research, application drafting, and budget generation. Spearheading expansion of the Financial Wellness Champion Training to state, county, local government, colleges, and university systems across Wisconsin. Established a sustainable teacher-led adoption model that scales without proportional marketing costs.

## ELEVATE Wisconsin — Impact & Key Learning
By providing financial education before students graduate high school, ELEVATE Wisconsin prepares them to make smart financial decisions when they receive distributions from tribal governments or enter the workforce. The program demonstrated that in EdTech, success requires understanding the true buyer (teachers and administrators), not just the end user (students) — a B2B2C insight foundational to the product strategy.
